## Limits

Native scoped effects run on the interpreter; strict VM coverage is for the pure
state/renderer and replay controls. The existing runtime does not encode the
adapter's effectful @limit scope for bytecode, so no whole native-loop VM claim is
made. AI waits synchronously and shows an honest waiting screen. This preparation
watch has no arrival, alien negotiation, asynchronous inbox or completed journey.
The unchanged8MiB journal/record bounds and formatter limits remain experimental
lab limits, not evidence of whole-journey scaling. Existing profile, cache schema,
model pin and authored simulation effects are preserved.
